Your CSV should follow the UNSW-NB15 feature schema. The following columns are expected (case-insensitive):

dur, proto, service, state, spkts, dpkts, sbytes, dbytes, rate, sttl, dttl, sload, dload, sloss, dloss, sinpkt, dinpkt, sjit, djit, swin, stcpb, dtcpb, dwin, tcprtt, synack, ackdat, smean, dmean, trans_depth, response_body_len, ct_srv_src, ct_state_ttl, ct_dst_ltm, ct_src_dport_ltm, ct_dst_sport_ltm, ct_dst_src_ltm, is_ftp_login, ct_ftp_cmd, ct_flw_http_mthd, ct_src_ltm, ct_srv_dst, is_sm_ips_ports

Note: The id, attack_cat, and label columns are automatically ignored if present.

  • Use a slice of the original UNSW-NB15 testing CSV for best accuracy.
  • The first row must be a header row with column names.
  • Missing values are handled automatically (filled with median/mode).
  • Rows with all-zero features may be classified as Normal.
